# Cohort Contact Sheet

> **Day 5 handout.** A blank sheet to keep the cohort connected after the academy. Sharing your details is **entirely optional** — fill in only what you're comfortable making visible to the rest of the cohort. The instructor circulates the compiled version only to those who opt in.

---

## How to fill this in

- Add your row. Leave any field blank you'd rather not share.
- **Opt-in** means: "Yes, include my row in the shared contact sheet." If it's "No," your row is not circulated.
- Prefer a work email, a personal email, or LinkedIn — your choice; use the "Best way to reach me" column to say which.
