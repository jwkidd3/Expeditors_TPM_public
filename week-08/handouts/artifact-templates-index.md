# Artifact Templates — Master Index

> **Day 5 handout.** Your take-home template pack. Every artifact template the academy taught, one line of purpose each, and the week it came from — so you can pull the right one in your next role without re-deriving it. The Week-8 "-light" variants are the compressed versions you produced under the 4-day capstone clock.

---

## Discovery + product definition

| Template | Purpose | From |
|---|---|---|
| Persona (validation canvas) | Name the role, job-to-be-done, and specific friction — grounded in real signal | Week 1 |
| Problem statement | One scoped problem an engineer can scope from without a clarifying call | Week 1 |
| Pain-point severity matrix | Rank pains on Severity × Frequency × Addressability | Week 1 |
| Evidence brief | Tag every claim by tier: Observed > Reported > Inferred > AI-generated | Week 1 |

| Template | Purpose | From |
