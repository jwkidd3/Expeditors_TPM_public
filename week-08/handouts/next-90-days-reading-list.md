# Next 90 Days — Reading, Listening, Watching

> **Day 5 handout.** A short, curated list to keep the muscles warm after the academy. Not a syllabus — pick 2 that match a gap you felt this week and go deep. Every item is a well-known, credible source in the product/TPM world.

---

## Books (pick 2)

- ***Inspired: How to Create Tech Products Customers Love* — Marty Cagan.** The reference text on modern product management: discovery, product teams, and outcome-over-output thinking. Read this if the "output ≠ outcome" idea is one you're keeping.
- ***Empowered: Ordinary People, Extraordinary Products* — Marty Cagan & Chris Jones.** The follow-up: how leaders and PMs actually operate in empowered teams. Read this if you're moving toward leading, not just building.
- ***Continuous Discovery Habits* — Teresa Torres.** A practical system for talking to customers every week and mapping opportunities to solutions. Read this if the discovery-and-persona work was your favorite part.
- ***Escaping the Build Trap* — Melissa Perri.** How organizations get stuck shipping features instead of delivering value — and how to escape. Read this if you're retiring the "ship features" reflex.
- ***Shape Up* — Ryan Singer (Basecamp, free online).** A concrete alternative to endless sprints: fixed time, variable scope, betting on shaped work. Read this if the delivery-and-bottleneck week resonated.

## Podcasts (subscribe to 1)

- ***Lenny's Podcast* — Lenny Rachitsky.** Long-form interviews with senior product and growth leaders; the closest thing to a standing PM seminar.
- ***Product Thinking* — Melissa Perri.** Shorter, sharper episodes on the craft and the hard organizational realities of product work.

## Talks (watch 1 this month)

- **"Escaping the Build Trap" — Melissa Perri** (conference talk; on YouTube). A 30-minute version of the book's core argument — a good, fast reset on outcome thinking.
- **Marty Cagan on product discovery** (SVPG talks and interviews; on YouTube). Any of his recorded sessions reinforce the discovery discipline from Weeks 1–3.

---

### How to use this list
1. Name the **one muscle you most want to deepen** (from your Day-5 reflection).
2. Pick the **single item** above that maps to it.
3. Put a **90-day calendar reminder** to note what changed in how you work. That's the loop that makes reading stick.
