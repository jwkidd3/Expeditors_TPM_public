# SEP-Light Template (1 page)

> **Day 4 handout.** The compressed Stakeholder Engagement Plan — Week-6 work in ~20 minutes. Stakeholder map + one trade-off brief + one simulated negotiation. The trade-off brief must be in the **stakeholder's currency**, not technical.

---

```markdown
# SEP-light — <feature>

## 1. Stakeholder map (compressed)
3 high-power stakeholders. Power × Interest placement + RACI for 1 decision (the most consequential).

## 2. 1 Trade-off brief
1 page. Pick one TCD-light §5 trade-off; brief for the relevant stakeholder.

## 3. 1 Simulated negotiation outcome
For one of the 5-most-consequential asks, document:
- The ask
- Anticipated stakeholder response (top 1 objection + your response)
- Anticipated agreement / deferral / escalation path
```

---

### Build order (20 min)
1. **5 min** — 3 stakeholders + RACI for the most consequential decision.
2. **10 min** — 1 trade-off brief (pull from TCD-light §5).
3. **5 min** — 1 simulated negotiation outcome.

### What "good" looks like
- The trade-off brief speaks in the **stakeholder's currency** — cost, risk, timeline, customer impact — not architecture jargon.
- The RACI names a **real, consequential decision**, not a generic one.
- The simulated negotiation anticipates the **single most likely objection** and has a response ready.
