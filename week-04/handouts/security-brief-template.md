### What "good" looks like

- The brief is **short** — a security partner can read it in 5 minutes.
- The "open questions" list is **specific**, not "tell us about security."
- The "decisions already made" section shows you made a starting point and want validation, not that you're outsourcing thinking.

> "Tell us about security" is a question that surrenders the conversation. Ask about specific scopes, controls, and precedents.
