# Stance Stamp Sheet — Four Stances

> **Day 1 · Activity 1 handout.** Record your quad's stance for each of the 8 triage cards. One stamp per card, plus a one-line reason grounded in the three-question frame.

---

### The four stances

| Stamp | Use when |
|-------|----------|
| 🟦 **Monolith fits** | One team, uniform load, no independent failure domain needed |
| 🟩 **Modular monolith fits** | One team now, but boundaries worth enforcing for later extraction |
| 🟪 **Microservice fits** | Two or more of the three questions answer "yes, with evidence" |
| ⬜ **Need more info** | Two or more answers are "we don't know" |
