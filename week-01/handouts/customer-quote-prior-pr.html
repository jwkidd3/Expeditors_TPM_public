Composite Customer Quote — From a Prior FieldPulse PR

Day 2 ambient context (Working Backwards). This is a real-style customer quote lifted from an earlier FieldPulse press release — the one written when the mobile Dispatch Board first shipped two years ago. It is composite and anonymized: stitched from several real customer conversations into one believable voice, with the name and shop changed. Use it as a model of a grounded, in-character quote — and as a caution about the alternative.

The shipped feature it came from

Mobile Dispatch Board (v1) — the release that let dispatchers see and re-assign the day’s jobs from a tablet instead of a wall whiteboard. It’s the feature that put FieldPulse on the map with mid-market shops.

The quote as it ran in the PR

“I ran my board on a dry-erase wall for nine years. The morning we switched, my youngest tech called out sick at 7:40 and I moved his four jobs to two other trucks before I’d finished my coffee — without walking to the wall or calling anybody. I didn’t realize how much of my day was spent just keeping the picture in my head until FieldPulse held it for me.”

— “Dana,” lead dispatcher, 70-technician HVAC shop (composite; details anonymized)

Why this quote works (and what to notice)

  • It names a specific moment (“called out sick at 7:40,” “moved his four jobs”) — not a benefit in the abstract.
  • It’s in a dispatcher’s own voice — “keeping the picture in my head,” “before I’d finished my coffee.” No adjectives, no marketing.
  • It describes the old workaround (the dry-erase wall) so the change is legible.
  • You could check it against a real person — a dispatcher would either recognize this or not.

The anti-example (what a weak quote sounds like)

“FieldPulse’s revolutionary, intuitive platform empowers dispatchers to seamlessly optimize their workflows and unlock next-level operational efficiency.”

No one says this. It’s un-checkable, it names no moment, and it could describe any product. If your Day 2 PR quote drifts toward this, read it aloud in the dispatcher’s voice — the mismatch will be obvious.

Attribution note: FieldPulse anonymizes customer quotes by changing the name, the shop size within a band, and any identifying operational detail, while preserving the moment and the voice. Treat this as the standard your own PR/FAQ customer quote will be held to.
