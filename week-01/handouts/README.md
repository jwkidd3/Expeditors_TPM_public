# Week 1 Handouts — FieldPulse Ambient Context

Participant-facing handouts for the Week 1 running case study (**FieldPulse**). These are the "ambient context" the Day 1 activity packet promises. Each is provided in **md / html / pdf**. Distribute (print or share) before the day that uses it.

| Handout | Introduced | Used in | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fieldpulse-product-overview` | Day 1 | All week | One-page product overview — orients every triad on the same product |
| `customer-quote-prior-pr` | Day 1 | Day 2 (Working Backwards) | Composite customer quote from an earlier FieldPulse PR — a model of a grounded, in-character quote |
| `dispatcher-interview-transcripts` | Day 1 | Day 3 (Personas) | Three anonymized dispatcher interviews (Maria R., Trey W., Susana O.) + two ride-along field notes |
| `support-tickets` | Day 1 | Day 4 (Pain Points) | 14 sanitized support tickets, from severe-and-frequent to noise |
| `analytics-export` | Day 1 | Day 3 + Day 4 | Section time-in-app (Day 3) + the end-of-day reconcile funnel with 65% step-4 abandonment (Day 4) |

**Design principle:** these handouts *provide evidence, not conclusions*. They deliberately do not diagnose pains, rank problems, or propose features — surfacing and scoring those is the participants' work across the week. Characters, numbers, and product facts are kept consistent across all five so cross-referencing them (a quote against a ticket against the funnel) is a valid exercise.
