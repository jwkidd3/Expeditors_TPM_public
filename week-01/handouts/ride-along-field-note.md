# Ride-Along Field Note — Maria R., Tuesday Shift

> **Day 3 · FieldPulse Research Packet.** Raw observation notes from one shift shadowing **Maria R., Lead Dispatcher, Reliable Climate Co. (90 technicians, 12 years dispatching).** Observed behavior only — no interpretation. Use as Observed-tier evidence when building your persona canvas.

**Observer:** Product researcher, FieldPulse Dispatcher Workflow squad
**Setting:** Branch dispatch desk, two monitors + desk phone + a personal paper notebook

---

## Timeline

- **06:40** — Arrives 20 min before first dispatch. Opens FieldPulse dashboard *and* a spiral notebook. Copies today's truck-stock numbers from the app into the notebook by hand before doing anything else.
- **06:55** — First re-route: a tech calls out sick. Maria reassigns 6 jobs in ~9 minutes, flipping between the map view and a **paper map taped beside the monitor**. Does not use the app's in-tool routing suggestion.
- **09:20** — Mid-morning re-route (customer no-show). Visibly tenses; says under her breath "here we go." Reassigns from memory, then updates the app after the fact.
- **11:30** — Asked (unprompted) why the notebook: "The inventory screen is *three clicks deep* and I need it in two seconds when a tech's standing on a roof. Faster to keep my own." Continues using the notebook as source of truth for truck stock all day.
- **12:15** — Two techs message asking "does dispatch know where my truck is?" Maria answers both by phone, not in-app.
- **14:45** — Second sick-call re-route of the day. Third re-route event overall.
- **17:05** — Shift "ends." Maria stays at the desk.
- **17:05–17:52** — **Post-shift reconcile.** Retypes the day's paper tickets into the system, cross-checks the notebook truck-stock against the app, resolves two mismatches by hand, submits the day. **47 minutes.**
- **17:52** — Leaves. Comments: "That's a normal night."
