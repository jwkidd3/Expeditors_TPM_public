Support Ticket Pack (14) — FieldPulse Pain Extraction Packet

Ambient context introduced Day 1; used on Day 4 (Pain Points). Fourteen sanitized support tickets from FieldPulse customers, spanning “the app crashed and I lost my night” to “please add dark mode.” Not pre-sorted, not pre-scored. Some are severe and frequent; some are noise; some describe pain FieldPulse can’t fully fix. Your job on Day 4 is to extract the real pains, find root causes, and score them on Severity × Frequency × Addressability — so resist the urge to rank them here.

Sanitized: customer names, shop names, and identifying details removed. “Reported frequency” is the customer’s words, not a measurement.

Ticket detail

T-01 — App crashes during end-of-day reconcile. “Three nights this week the app froze while I was closing out the day and I had to start the reconcile over. Lost about half an hour each time. I’ve started keeping the paper tickets just in case.” (90-tech HVAC dispatcher)

T-02 — Can’t submit a ticket from the field on a weak signal. “My techs can’t submit their tickets when they’re in a basement or out past cell coverage. So they write it on paper and hand it to the dispatcher, who types it in at night. Defeats the purpose of the mobile app.” (60-tech HVAC office manager)

T-03 — Reconcile flow is too long; staff give up partway. “There are too many steps to close out a job. My dispatcher gets to the parts-used step and just bails, then does the rest on a spreadsheet. We’re paying for the software and still using a spreadsheet.” (160-tech HVAC ops lead)

T-04 — Truck inventory counts are wrong by end of week. “The inventory in the app never matches what’s actually on the trucks by Thursday or Friday. We usually catch it at payroll when the numbers don’t add up. Nobody trusts the inventory screen anymore.” (90-tech plumbing owner)

T-05 — New dispatchers can’t get through the setup screens. “Every new dispatcher I hire gets stuck in the initial setup. There’s a screen a few steps in where they just stop and come ask me. I’ve started doing the setup for them.” (110-tech HVAC lead dispatcher)

T-06 — Mid-day reassign is slow; board lags the change. “When someone calls out and I reassign their jobs, the board takes a minute to catch up, so I call the techs to be sure. By then I’ve done it twice — once on the phone, once on the screen.” (75-tech electrical dispatcher)

T-07 — In-app messages are delayed when it’s busy. “The in-app chat is fine when it’s quiet. On a busy morning the messages lag, so I just text my techs on their personal phones. At least I know those go through.” (60-tech HVAC dispatcher)

T-08 — Customer got the wrong arrival-window text after a reschedule. “We moved a job and the customer got an automated text with the old time. They were waiting the wrong two hours and were not happy. It doesn’t happen often but it’s embarrassing when it does.” (130-tech HVAC CSR)

T-09 — Owner report is missing a ‘completed vs billed’ column. “The Friday report shows completed jobs but not whether they were billed. That’s the one number my owner asks about, so I rebuild it by hand every week.” (160-tech HVAC dispatcher)

T-10 — Job-change push notifications arrive late on the tech app. “When I change a job, the tech doesn’t always get the notification right away. A couple times a tech has driven to the wrong address because the update came in late.” (80-tech plumbing dispatcher)

T-11 — Please add dark mode. “Would be nice to have a dark mode for the dispatcher screen, it’s a little bright in our office. Not urgent.” (55-tech HVAC dispatcher)

T-12 — Map view doesn’t show real-time truck location reliably. “The map is supposed to show where the trucks are but it’s often stale or blank. I’ve mostly stopped using it and just call to ask where they are.” (140-tech HVAC dispatcher)

T-13 — Can’t log in after our IT changed the password policy. “Our IT department tightened our password rules and none of us could log in for most of a day. Support sorted it out but we lost the morning.” (200-tech mechanical contractor)

T-14 — Please integrate with our accounting software. “We’d love it if FieldPulse talked to our accounting system so we’re not re-entering invoices. I know it’s a big ask.” (70-tech HVAC owner)
