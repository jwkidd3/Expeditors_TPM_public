> **Day 4 · Activity 2 handout.** Place each promoted pain on the grid by **Severity** (how bad when it happens) × **Frequency** (how often it happens). Then **color-code each by Addressability** — how much of it we can actually engineer away.
>
> **Addressability key:** 🟢 Engineer-fixable · 🟡 Partly fixable / needs design · 🔴 Must communicate/live with (not a build fix)

### Reading the grid
- **High × High** = fix first (your Top 3 usually live here).
- **High severity × Low frequency** = rare but painful — decide: engineer or communicate?
- **Low × Low** = deprioritize; don't let these crowd the readout.
