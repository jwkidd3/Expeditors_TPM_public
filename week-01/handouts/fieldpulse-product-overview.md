## What FieldPulse is

**FieldPulse** is a mobile-first **field-service dispatch SaaS**. It helps service businesses schedule jobs, dispatch technicians, and keep the office and the field in sync throughout the day. Two applications make up the core product:

- **Dispatcher web app** — where the office schedules jobs, assigns and re-assigns technicians, and tracks the day as it unfolds.
- **Technician mobile app** — where field techs see their route, job details, and customer history, and report work as they complete it.

## Who it's for

FieldPulse is sold to **HVAC, plumbing, and electrical shops** running roughly **50–200 technicians** — large enough to feel real scheduling pain, small enough to lack a custom-built internal platform. It competes with **ServiceTitan**, **Housecall Pro**, and the **in-house tools and spreadsheets** these shops often cobble together themselves.

## The people who use it every day

| Role | What they do with FieldPulse | Why they matter to us |
|---|---|---|
| **Dispatcher** | Builds the daily schedule, reacts to changes (call-outs, cancellations, emergencies), keeps promised arrival windows | The primary user of our squad's product surface |
| **Technician** | Follows the route, works the jobs, reports status from the field | Success depends on the mobile app working in real-world field conditions |
| **Shop owner / ops lead** | Watches the numbers, reports results, decides what to buy | Holds the budget and renews (or churns) the account |

## What leadership is asking right now

Framed as open questions — not answers — to orient your week:

- Are dispatchers actually able to keep their promised arrival windows on a normal, messy day?
- Where does the handoff between the office and the field break down?
- What do dispatchers and technicians *do outside the product* to get their jobs done — and what does that tell us?
- Which of our numbers would a shop owner point to when deciding whether to renew?
