# Working Backwards — Four Vignettes

> **Day 2 · Activity 1 handout.** Four short accounts of how a product was conceived. For each, decide: was this **forward-built** (tool/feature first) or **backward-built** (customer outcome first)? Name the evidence. Two of the four are designed to mislead — read carefully. For each, write the **one question** that, if asked earlier, would have changed the outcome.

---

## Vignette A
A dispatch-tool founder, after hearing customers complain about mobile app speed, built a native iOS rewrite over two quarters. At launch, 40% of techs still used the web view because they'd switched to Android mid-project.

## Vignette B
A PM wrote a one-page mock press release for "OfflineFirst Ticket Capture" and circulated it to five techs for reaction. Three said they'd already built paper workarounds; two said the app's login flow was the real blocker. The PR was re-scoped to login before offline.

## Vignette C
Engineering discovered a faster map-tile rendering library and shipped it as a mid-sprint improvement. Dispatcher satisfaction bumped 12 points in the next survey.

## Vignette D
A PM drafted a PR/FAQ for "Tech-First Messaging" and had AI generate three customer quotes. The quotes all used marketing language no tech would say. The PM skipped persona research and shipped anyway.

---

### For each vignette
1. **Forward-built or backward-built?**
2. **What's the evidence?**
3. **The one question** that, asked earlier, would have changed the outcome: ______________________
