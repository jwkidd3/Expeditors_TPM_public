# Engagement Style by Quadrant

> **Day 2 · Activity 1 handout.** Turns yesterday's Power × Interest placement into a default engagement style — cadence, format, length. Starting points to calibrate per stakeholder, not rigid rules.

---

## The four engagement principles

| Principle | Plain English |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Engage early** | Surprise = anger. Surface concerns before they become decisions. |
| **Engage deliberately** | One channel per stakeholder. Avoid the Slack-DM-plus-email-plus-meeting redundancy. |
| **Match form to power** | High-power stakeholders get crisp written updates; high-interest stakeholders get conversation. |
| **Bidirectional asks** | Every engagement names what *they* need from *us* AND what *we* need from *them*. |

Most TPMs do (1) and (2) well by their second quarter in the role. (3) and (4) are the differentiators.

## Engagement style by quadrant

| Quadrant | Cadence | Format | Length |
|----------|---------|--------|--------|
| **High Power + High Interest** | Weekly or bi-weekly | Written brief + 30-min sync | Brief: 1 page; sync: focused agenda |
| **High Power + Low Interest** | Monthly or trigger-based | Crisp written update; rarely a sync | < 250 words |
| **Low Power + High Interest** | Bi-weekly | Email digest; group thread | 5 bullet points |
| **Low Power + Low Interest** | Quarterly or on-launch | Newsletter / launch email | Short |

These are starting points. Adjust for personality — a terse VP may want fewer words than the table implies; a nervous partner-team lead may need more contact than the quadrant suggests.

## Calibration reminders

- **Crispest writing** goes to the **high-power / low-interest** quadrant — they will read 250 words, never 20 pages.
- **Group channels** (digests, threads) serve **low-power / high-interest** cohorts — engage them together, not 1:1.
- **Most of your time** should land on **high-power / high-interest** partners. If it doesn't, you're mis-investing.
- **No invented meetings.** The plan must respect calendar reality.
