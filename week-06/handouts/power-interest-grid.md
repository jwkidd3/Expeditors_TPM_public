# Power × Interest Grid

> **Day 1 · Activity 2 handout.** The 2×2 frame for placing every stakeholder by how much they can affect the work (power) and how much they care about it (interest). Sketch this on a whiteboard or print it and place a sticky per stakeholder.

---

## The grid

```
                            High Interest
                                  ▲
                                  │
        Keep informed             │      Manage closely
        (engaged audience;        │      (key partners; frequent,
         you'll learn from them)  │       deep engagement)
                                  │
─────High Power ──────────────────┼─────────────────── Low Power
                                  │
        Manage with authority     │      Monitor
        (keep out of the weeds;   │      (low effort; check in
         surface only when needed)│       periodically)
                                  │
                                  ▼
                            Low Interest
```

## What each axis means

- **Power** — how much can they affect this work? Can they block it, fund it, redirect it, accelerate it?
- **Interest** — how much do they care whether this ships?

## Engagement style by quadrant

| Quadrant | Engagement style |
|----------|------------------|
| **High power, high interest** | Manage closely — frequent, deep engagement |
| **High power, low interest** | Manage with authority — keep them out of the weeds; surface only when needed |
| **Low power, high interest** | Keep informed — engaged audience; you'll learn from them |
| **Low power, low interest** | Monitor — low effort; check in periodically |

## The quadrant that bites you

Most TPMs over-invest in the high-interest quadrants and under-invest in **high-power / low-interest**. That is the quadrant that surprises you with a "no" at the worst possible time. A stakeholder here can shift into high-interest the moment their trigger fires (an audit deadline, a cost spike, a top-account escalation) — see the watch list.

## Worked example — FieldPulse "End-of-Day Reconcile"

```
                            High Interest
                                  ▲
                                  │
    [Operations VP] ←──────────── │ ──── [Reconcile squad eng lead]
                                  │ ───── [Customer Success lead]
    Keep informed                 │      [QA lead]
                                  │      Manage closely
                                  │
─────High Power ──────────────────┼─────────────────── Low Power
                                  │
    [Architect]                   │      [Compliance owner]
    [Eng Director]                │      [Other product-team owners]
    [Security Lead]               │      Monitor
    Manage with authority         │
                                  │
                                  ▼
                            Low Interest
```

The FieldPulse "surprise": **Compliance** looks low-interest in the abstract but becomes **high-power in specific moments** (audit deadlines). Its position can shift — flag it on the watch list.
