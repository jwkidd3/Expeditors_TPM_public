# Engagement Plan Template

> **Day 2 · Activity 2 handout.** One block per stakeholder. Capture the recurring engagement (stable month-to-month) and the "right now" asks (what changes this period). This becomes SEP Section 2.

---

## Per-stakeholder block

```markdown
### <Name / Role>

**Quadrant:** High-Power × Low-Interest (etc.)

**Cadence:** weekly / bi-weekly / monthly / quarterly / trigger-based

**Format:** crisp memo / email / sync / standup mention / newsletter

**Length:** < N words / < N min

**What they need from us (recurring):**
- ...

**What we need from them (recurring):**
- ...

**What they need from us right now (this period):**
- ...

**What we need from them right now (this period):**
- ...

**Channel of choice:** email / Slack / sync / Confluence
**Backup channel:** ...

**Stakeholder-specific notes:**
- (preferences, sensitivities, history with similar work)
```

The **"recurring"** rows are stable. The **"right now"** rows are what you refresh each period.

## The "right now" asks — three categories

Every "right now" ask is a Decision, Information, or Action ask. Predict the reciprocal ask too — what *they* will ask of *you*.

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Decisions** | Confirm scope; sign off on architecture stance; approve SLO targets |
| **Information** | Answer a specific question; share a doc; introduce someone |
| **Action** | Add a story to their team's backlog; schedule a review; make a call |

Specific beats vague: "approve the SLO" beats "review the doc."

## Engagement anti-patterns — self-diagnostic

Rate your quad H / M / L on susceptibility to each, then bake a counter-measure into the plan.

| Anti-pattern | Symptom | Counter-measure |
|--------------|---------|-----------------|
| **Channel sprawl** | Same message via Slack DM + email + meeting | Commit to one channel of record per stakeholder |
| **Rolling silence** | Stakeholder untouched for 6 weeks; then a problem | Calendar each cadence as a recurring reminder |
| **Surprise reveal** | Major decision shared after the fact | Engage early; pre-share before the public moment |
| **Asymmetric asks** | TPM only asks; never offers | Build "what we offer" into each engagement |
| **Veto avoidance** | TPM avoids the high-power / low-interest stakeholder | Trigger-based engagement from the watch list |
| **Email avalanche** | 20-page deck to a 250-word audience | Match length to audience |
