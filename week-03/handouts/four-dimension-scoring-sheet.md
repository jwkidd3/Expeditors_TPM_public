# Four-Dimension Scoring Sheet

> **Day 1 · Activity 1 handout.** Score each of your three Week-2 feature cards on four dimensions (1–5 each). Highest total is the default pick; if scores tie, your quad must argue it out. Fillable — one row per feature.

---

## The four dimensions (1 = low, 5 = high)

| Dimension | Question you're scoring |
|-----------|-------------------------|
| **Friction severity** | How big is the pain it addresses? |
| **Metric movability** | How confident are we it'll move the metric within 30 days? |
| **Build feasibility** | How achievable in one quarter? |
| **Learning value** | What do we learn even if it doesn't ship? |
