# Secondary Review Note Template

> **Day 5 · Activity 4 handout.** The afternoon deliverable. A *different* reviewer reads only the **revised** PRD and its resolution log — not the originals. They honesty-check the log against the actual changes, catch new problems, judge the deferrals, and issue the final verdict.

---

```markdown
# Secondary Review of PRD: <feature name>
**Reviewer:** <triad>  |  **Authors:** <triad>

## Final score: [weighted total]

## What improved (vs. AM scores)
- …

## What still needs work (gaps in the revision)
- …

## Verdict
- [ ] Ships as-is (score ≥ 3.0)
- [ ] Ships with named gaps (score 2.0–2.9, gaps captured in resolution log)
- [ ] Needs further revision (score < 2.0; facilitator review)
```

---

## What the secondary reviewer checks

1. **Honesty check** — does the resolution log line up with the *actual* changes in the PRD? (This is the point of the round — don't skip it.)
2. **New problems** — did the revisions introduce anything new?
3. **Deferrals** — are they reasonable, and tracked somewhere?
4. **Final score** — score with the Friday rubric; compare against the AM scores in the log. Did the PRD genuinely improve?

---

## Sign-off

Hand the note to the authors. They sign off — or, if the verdict is **Needs further revision (< 2.0)**, escalate to the facilitator before wrap. Verdicts below 2.0 must not slip past close.
