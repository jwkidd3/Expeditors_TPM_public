# Week 3 · Day 5 — PRD Review Run Sheet (Virtual) · Facilitator

> Companion to `labs/day-05-prd-review.md`. Facilitator-only run-of-day for the **virtual** delivery — pairing rotation, room sequence, timing. Ships in the facilitator folder of every repo (it carries no participant data).
>
> **Mini-capstone deliverable · non-AI.** 5 quads, each with its own Zoom **breakout room**. **Main room = full-group only (Opening, Wrap).** The morning is a sequence of review sessions — one PRD at a time; the afternoon is parallel work.

## Timeline

| Order | Activity | Length |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opening — *main room* | 15 min |
| 2 | Primary review — Sessions 1–5 | 150 min |
| 3 | Revisions — *all breakout rooms at once* | 50 min |
| 4 | Secondary review — Sessions 1–5 | 60 min |
| 5 | Wrap — *main room* | 15 min |

---

## 1. Opening — 15 min · Main room
Whole group. Share the pairings, the rubric, and the non-AI rule.

## 2. Primary review — 5 sessions, one PRD at a time, in order
Each PRD gets the floor in turn, **in the author's breakout room**. The author presents; its two reviewers score and discuss. About **30 min per session**.

| Session | Presenting (author) | Reviewing |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Aurora | Basalt & Cascade |
| **2** | Basalt | Cascade & Delta |
| **3** | Cascade | Delta & Everest |
| **4** | Delta | Everest & Aurora |
| **5** | Everest | Aurora & Basalt |

**Each session (~30 min):**
1. Author **presents** the PRD (~5 min).
2. Reviewers **score** it against the rubric (~15 min).
3. **Clarifying discussion** — reviewers give findings; author captures adopt / defer / push-back (~10 min).

*Quads not in the current session wait in their own room (re-read the PRD they'll review next, or start notes).*

## 3. Revisions — 50 min · all breakout rooms at once
Every quad works in its **own** room, revising its PRD from the feedback and updating its resolution log. Flip status to "Revised." (All 5 in parallel.)

## 4. Secondary review — 5 sessions, one PRD at a time, in order
Each author presents its **revised** PRD to its secondary reviewer, who scores and signs off. About **12 min per session**, in the author's room. **Every secondary reviewer is fresh eyes** — a quad that did *not* do the primary review on that PRD.

| Session | Presenting (author) | Secondary reviewer |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Aurora | Delta |
| **2** | Basalt | Everest |
| **3** | Cascade | Aurora |
| **4** | Delta | Basalt |
| **5** | Everest | Cascade |

## 5. Wrap — 15 min · Main room
Whole group. Each quad delivers: **approved PRD + resolution log + secondary sign-off.**

---

## Why the morning is sequential
Each review session needs the author plus its two reviewers together — 3 of the 5 quads. Two sessions at once would need 6 quads, so only **one PRD is reviewed at a time**. Revisions (Step 3) are solo, so those run all at once.

## Pairing rotation (reusable for any 5 quads)
Label the quads 1–5 in a ring. Each PRD's **primary reviewers** are the next two quads (+1, +2); its **secondary reviewer** is the third (+3) — guaranteeing the secondary never overlaps the primaries. (Rubric lives in `labs/day-05-prd-review.md` and the `friday-review-rubric` handout.)
