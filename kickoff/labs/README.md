# Kickoff — Half-Day Orientation

> *"An eight-week academy without a shared starting line tends to drift in week one. Today is the starting line."*

The kickoff is a **half-day pre-Week-1 orientation** that produces three concrete outputs: a cohort that knows each other by face and story, formed quads with written commitments, and confirmed technical readiness for Monday.

This session is **the only time** the full eight-week structure is surveyed before participants are deep in it. Treat the curriculum tour as orientation, not preview — the goal is location and direction, not content.

**Run [`../../instructor/markdown/presentations/instructor.md`](../../instructor/markdown/presentations/instructor.md) before this deck** — it covers the instructor intro, ground rules, and notetaker policy. The kickoff deck assumes those slides have already been delivered.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the half-day, each participant can:

1. State in one sentence what a Technical Product Manager owns that a Product Manager or Engineering Manager does not.
2. Name the **five sibling artifacts** (PRD, TCD, TMD, SEP, DP) and which two are produced during the academy's capstone moments.
3. Locate themselves on the eight-week map — what each week's theme is and which two weeks contain a capstone deliverable.
4. Identify their **cohort quad** by name and recite the quad's written commitment.
5. List the AI policy: which week is AI-off, what a provenance note is, and why both exist.

## Half-day schedule

| Clock | Block | Mix |
|-------|-------|-----|
| 09:00 – 09:30 | Welcome + Activity 1 (Two Truths and a TPM) | 10 min facilitator / 20 min pairs |
| 09:30 – 10:30 | Block 1 — The TPM Role + Activity 2 | 30 min teach / 30 min quartets |
| 10:30 – 10:45 | **Break** | |
| 10:45 – 11:45 | Block 2 — Curriculum Tour + Activity 3 (Quad Formation) | 35 min teach / 25 min quads |
| 11:45 – 12:30 | Logistics, Q&A, Week 1 launch | 45 min full room |

**Group composition:** the morning moves through three group shapes — pairs (Activity 1), quartets (Activity 2), quads (Activity 3, which then persist through Week 3).

## Facilitator pre-flight checklist

- [ ] Confirm room/setup: side monitor for the schedule slide, on-screen timer for breakouts, projector for the deck.
- [ ] Bring **index cards and Sharpies** — needed for Activity 3 quad commitments.
- [ ] Print or share digitally: the [Activity packet](./kickoff-activities.md).
- [ ] Pre-decide answers to common Q&A: missed-day policy, quad change policy, performance evaluation, artifact retention.
- [ ] Verify the cohort roster — flag any pre-work or SkillIQ pre-assessment gaps before they walk in.
- [ ] Have the running case study (FieldPulse) one-pager ready as a Monday hand-off, even though it isn't used today.

## What "done" looks like at 12:30

- Every participant has a named quad and a written quad commitment card
- Every participant knows the daily rhythm and the AI policy
- Every participant has flagged any access/setup gaps for Monday
- The instructor has noted any cohort-level signals (energy, anxiety, gaps) to adjust Week 1 Day 1 opening

## Common pitfalls

1. **Letting introductions sprawl.** Round-the-room introductions for 12+ participants eats 45 minutes. Use Activity 1 (pairs) instead and surface one highlight per pair.
2. **Skipping quad formation pressure.** Quads chosen by friendship rarely have the cognitive diversity that Week 3's PRD needs. Enforce the "at least one different dimension" rule.
3. **Treating the curriculum tour as a syllabus read-aloud.** The eight-week table is a map, not a lecture. Spend more time on the *why* of Week 3 AI-off and the Week 8 capstone than on weekly themes.
4. **Holding Q&A until the end.** Take live questions throughout; reserve the final Q&A block for the harder ones that have surfaced through the morning.
