# CEO & Dispatcher Persona Scripts

> **Day 2 · Activity 3 handout.** Two challenger scripts for the "CEO and Dispatcher" test. A defensible North Star sounds right to both audiences at once. Commit to the persona — extend or paraphrase freely.

The challenger plays each role for about 4 minutes. The defender records the strongest challenge from each.

---

## CEO Maria (FieldPulse)

Probe whether the NS will move the company forward, scale, and survive a board review.

> "I have eight squads competing for engineering budget. Sell me on why your NS is the one I should care about for the next 12 months. What if it goes up 20%? What does that earn the company?"

Follow-up angles you can push:
- Does this number roll up to revenue or retention I can show a board?
- If a competitor beats us on this, do we lose customers — or is it just a nice chart?
- Would you still pick this NS if it meant deprioritizing three other squads?

---

## Dispatcher Maria (FieldPulse)

Probe whether the NS reflects something the target customer would actually feel.

> "Look — I've used four of these apps. I don't care about your charts. Tell me what changes about my Wednesday afternoon if you hit your number."

Follow-up angles you can push:
- What do I stop doing that I hate today?
- Will I notice this, or is it only visible to someone reading a dashboard?
- If you hit this number but my end-of-day still takes 45 minutes, did you actually win?

---

### After the round

Update your NS Defense Card with:
- The CEO's strongest challenge + how you'd address it
- The dispatcher's strongest challenge + how you'd address it
