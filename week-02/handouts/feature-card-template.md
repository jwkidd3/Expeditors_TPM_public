# Feature Card Template

> **Day 5 · Activity 3 handout.** Translate your highest-friction journey stages into three feature concepts that pass the strategy test. Brainstorm 8, test each, cull to 3.

## The strategy test

A feature concept earns a place on your map only if it passes all three:

- [ ] **Friction link** — names the specific stage and friction it would reduce
- [ ] **Metric link** — names the operational signal or KPI it would move
- [ ] **Principle link** — aligns with at least one of your 3 design principles (or names which it deliberately trades off)

Most candidates will fail one. That's the point of testing before culling.

---

## The card (fill one per surviving feature, three total)

```markdown
## Feature concept: <short name>

**Stage(s) addressed:** <journey stages>
**Friction reduced:** <specific friction starred on the map>
**Metric it moves:** <op-signal or KPI from your Tier Sheet>
**Aligned principle:** <which of the 3 from Day 3>
**Trade-off / risk:** <what we'd give up; what could go wrong>
**Validation:** how would we know it actually moved the metric in 30 days?
```

---

### Guardrails

- A feature with **no metric link** is the most common error. Force the link.
- If all three features address the **same stage**, ask whether that stage is really 80% of the friction.
- The **Trade-off / risk** box is non-negotiable. "No downside" is a failed answer.
