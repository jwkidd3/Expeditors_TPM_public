## How to cite

When you tag evidence in the Strategy Brief, name the document and the locator so any citation can be checked against source:

- Interviews → interviewee + quote number (e.g., "Interview — Maria R., Q3")
- Tickets → ticket ID (e.g., "Ticket T-03")
- Analyst report → section heading
- Competitor tours → competitor + timestamp
- Slack → channel + date (e.g., "#voice-of-customer, 2025-09-12")

## Evidence tiers (used course-wide)

> **Observed > Reported > Inferred > AI-generated.**

- **Observed** — a researcher watched it happen (ride-along notes, screen recordings).
- **Reported** — someone said it (interview quotes, ticket text, Slack messages).
- **Inferred** — a reasonable conclusion drawn across sources, not stated outright.
- **AI-generated** — produced by the model; unverified until you check it against a higher tier.

*All materials are sanitized and anonymized: customer and shop names removed, shop sizes preserved within a band, identifying details changed. Reported frequencies are the customer's words, not measurements. Nothing here is analyzed for you.*
