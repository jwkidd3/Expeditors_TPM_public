# Week-2 Readout Scoring Rubric

> **Day 5 · Activity 4 handout.** The live rubric peers and the instructor use to score each quad's 12-minute integrated strategy package. Use it to score others — and to pressure-test your own package before you present.

Score each dimension against the "exemplary" bar. Weighted total out of 100.

| Dimension | Weight | What "exemplary" looks like | Score |
|-----------|--------|------------------------------|-------|
| **Metric coherence** | 25% | NS, KPIs, and op-signals form a believable causal chain | |
| **North Star defense** | 20% | Quad can name + rebut the three pitfalls applied to *their own* NS | |
| **Journey realism** | 20% | Map references observed Week-1 pain points; non-product touchpoints present | |
| **Feature linkage** | 15% | Each top feature ties to journey friction AND a Tier Sheet metric | |
| **AI-use transparency** | 10% | Where AI summarized research, prompts and validation steps are disclosed | |
| **Design awareness** | 10% | At least one UX principle named with its trade-off acknowledged | |

## What to present (in order, 12 min)

1. **North Star + counter-metric** (1 min)
2. **Tier Sheet causal chain** (2 min) — three sentences
3. **One design principle in detail** (2 min) — the one you expect the most pushback on
4. **Journey map walk-through** (4 min) — narrate the persona's journey, friction stars
5. **3 feature concepts** (3 min) — each with stage, friction, metric, principle

Then 3 min Q&A.

### Rules that affect your score

- **Hold the clock.** Quads that blow their time get scored down on coherence — it's a real signal.
- **All three voices present.** A single designated speaker is not allowed.
- Predict the toughest peer challenge in advance and be ready for it.
