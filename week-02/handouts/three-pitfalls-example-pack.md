# Three Pitfalls — Reference + Example Pack

> **Day 2 · Activity 1 handout.** The three standard North Star pitfalls, plus eight anonymized public-company NS candidates to score before you diagnose your own.

## The three standard pitfalls

| Pitfall | What it looks like | The tell |
|---------|--------------------|----------|
| **1. Vanity dressed up** | A number that sounds north-star-ish but isn't | Customers don't feel it directly when this number moves |
| **2. Leading-indicator confusion** | A metric that's actually a KPI or op-signal in disguise | A team can move it without the customer being any better off long-term |
| **3. Gameable target** | A target with no usage-quality gate | A team can hit it with sales tactics or notification spam |

A well-formed North Star has been challenged against all three and survived.
