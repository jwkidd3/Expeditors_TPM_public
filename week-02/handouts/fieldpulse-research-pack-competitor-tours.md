# FieldPulse Research Pack — Competitor Product-Tour Notes

> **Day 4 · Research Pack (4 of 6).** Notes taken while watching two competitors' public product-tour videos, plus what their public pricing pages showed on the day of capture. This is the **only** competitor material you're allowed to feed the AI — the bright line is that AI can structure what you paste, but cannot tell you what competitors actually do. Where these notes are silent, the correct answer is "source is silent," not a guess.

*Composite, anonymized notes assembled for training. Timestamps are approximate positions in each tour. Pricing reflects what was publicly listed on the capture date and changes often. **Reported** evidence — a vendor describing its own product.*

## Tour 1 — ServiceTitan (product-tour walkthrough)

*Framing throughout: "the operating system for the trades." Aimed at larger, growth-minded shops.*

- **[00:40] Dispatch board.** Drag-and-drop board with capacity view. Narrator emphasizes "see your whole day and rebalance in seconds." Shows reassigning a job by dragging a card; the board is presented as updating live. *(No mention of sync latency or offline behavior.)*
- **[03:10] Mobile technician app.** Techs see their route, job details, and can capture photos, collect signatures, and take payment on-site. Narrator: "everything the tech needs, in their pocket." *(Offline capture is mentioned in passing — "keeps working when signal drops" — but not demonstrated.)*
- **[06:25] Pricebook & estimates.** Big emphasis on presenting good/better/best options to the customer at the door to drive ticket size. This is a headline feature.
- **[09:50] Reporting & financials.** Deep reporting: revenue by job type, technician performance, marketing ROI, "completed vs. invoiced" shown on a dashboard. Narrator frames reporting as a core reason to buy.
- **[13:15] Integrations.** Mentions accounting integration (QuickBooks named), payroll, and a marketplace of add-ons. *(Specifics of what syncs are not detailed in the tour.)*
- **[15:40] Onboarding.** Narrator references "a dedicated implementation team" and "guided onboarding." No demo of the setup flow itself. *(Source silent on how long implementation takes or how self-serve it is.)*

**Public pricing page (capture date):** No public per-seat price listed. Page directs prospects to "request a demo / get a custom quote." Positioning language: "built for growing trades businesses." *(Source silent on actual price.)*

## Tour 2 — Housecall Pro (product-tour walkthrough)

*Framing throughout: "run your business from your phone." Aimed at smaller shops and owner-operators.*

- **[00:30] Scheduling.** Clean calendar; drag a job to a tech. Emphasis on how fast and simple it is. Narrator: "book a job in seconds." *(No capacity/multi-crew rebalancing shown at scale.)*
- **[02:15] Mobile-first experience.** Strong emphasis on the phone experience for both office and tech. Consumer-grade polish is the selling point. *(Offline behavior not mentioned.)*
- **[04:05] Customer communication.** Automated appointment reminders, "on my way" texts, and follow-up review requests. This is a headline feature — heavy focus on the homeowner experience.
- **[06:40] Payments & invoicing.** Take payment in the field, send invoices, offer consumer financing. Narrator emphasizes getting paid faster.
- **[08:20] Reporting.** Basic dashboards — revenue, jobs completed, some technician stats. Presented as "the numbers you need at a glance." *(No "completed vs. billed" reconciliation view shown; source silent on advanced/multi-location reporting.)*
- **[09:30] Onboarding.** Narrator stresses "set up in minutes, no training required" and self-serve signup. A short setup flow is shown on screen.

**Public pricing page (capture date):** Tiered public pricing shown — a Basic, an Essentials, and a Max tier, billed per month with a per-additional-user add-on. Free trial offered. Positioning language: "the #1 rated app for home service pros." *(The specific dollar figures shown on the page are not reproduced here — check the live page; they change.)*
